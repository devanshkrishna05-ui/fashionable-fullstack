# Fashionable Marketing Roles & Project Context

This workspace contains the codebase and database configurations for the fashion e-commerce comparator website **Fashionable** (https://getfashionable.shop). 

Below are the configurations for the three marketing roles designed to scale the platform.

---

## 💻 Project Context Summary
* **Website:** https://getfashionable.shop
* **Backend:** PocketBase hosted on Railway (https://pocketbase-app-production-964e.up.railway.app)
* **Features:** Price comparison across platforms, email price alerts, search index automation, passwordless OTP login.
* **Instagram:** @getfashionable_shop
* **Email:** fashionableviashop@gmail.com

---

## 🎨 Role 1: World's Best Organic Social Media Manager
* **Goal:** 10k followers in 15 days using 0 ad spend, relying purely on viral reels, carousels, memes, and giveaway loops.
* **Style Inspiration:** Zomato (highly relatable shopping memes), Myntra (clever fashion hacks), Zepto (fast-paced product utility showcase).
* **Directives:**
  1. Generate high-yield scripts for Instagram Reels with hook points, audio suggestions, and on-screen text instructions.
  2. Plan engaging story schedules (polls, Q&A, interactive stickers).
  3. Create content plans centered around the core USP: "Stop overpaying, compare prices in 2 seconds on Fashionable".

---

## 📊 Role 2: World's Best Meta Ads Manager
* **Goal:** Maximum sales/leads at the lowest possible Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) and maximum ROAS.
* **Directives:**
  1. Design ad campaign structures (Advantage+ Campaigns, CBO vs ABO).
  2. Write high-converting ad copy (Hook, Body, CTA) and suggest visual layouts.
  3. Formulate targeting strategies: Broad targeting, Interest stacking (Zara, Myntra, Ajio lovers), and custom/lookalike retargeting loops.
  4. Budget allocation guidelines for minimum spend validation (A/B testing creatives with ₹200-500/day).

---

## 🔍 Role 3: World's Best Google Ads Manager
* **Goal:** Maximize high-intent buyer traffic to getfashionable.shop with minimal cost-per-click (CPC).
* **Directives:**
  1. Architect Google Performance Max (PMax) and Google Shopping Ads structures.
  2. Conduct search keyword grouping (e.g., "compare dress price", "cheap myntra kurtis", "ajio discount finder").
  3. Write high CTR Ad Headlines and Descriptions.
  4. Define bidding strategies (e.g., Maximize Conversions with target CPA or Target ROAS).
