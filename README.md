## 🌟 Key Product Features

- 🔍 **Real-Time Price Comparison**: Instantly compare fashion product prices across top Indian e-commerce platforms.
- 🔔 **Smart Price Drop Alerts**: Passwordless OTP authentication for instant email price reduction notifications.
- ⚡ **Automated Search Index**: High-speed catalog search and indexing engine.
- 📱 **Mobile-First Responsive UX**: High conversion rate (CRO) visual interface.

---

## 📊 Growth Marketing & Data Analytics Engine

Engineered with a data-first approach for customer acquisition, attribution, and retention:

### 1. 📈 Performance Marketing & Paid UA (Meta & Google Ads)
- **Meta Ads Architecture**: Custom CBO/ABO campaign structures targeting high-intent fashion shoppers (Zara, Myntra, Ajio interest stacks).
- **Google Search & PMax**: Performance Max campaigns targeting high-intent transactional search terms.
- **CAC & ROAS Optimization**: A/B testing ad creatives to minimize Cost Per Acquisition (CAC) and maximize Return On Ad Spend.

### 2. 🔍 SEO & Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)
- Structured schema metadata for search engines to capture high organic buyer traffic.
- High-converting funnel design reducing bounce rates on product comparison pages.

### 3. 🎯 Analytics & Tracking (GA4 & Event Attribution)
- Event tracking across the user journey (Search -> Compare -> Price Alert Signup -> Outbound Merchant Click).
- Funnel analysis to measure user retention and engagement loops.
